Considerations

Questions owners ask first.

Do I keep control of my property?

Always. You own it, set the boundaries, and approve the meaningful decisions. We carry the day-to-day.

Short-term or mid-term — how do you decide?

We begin with your local rules and the numbers. Where short-term is restricted or soft, mid-term furnished stays are often the steadier, stronger play.

What areas do you serve?

San Bernardino and Los Angeles County — both coastal and mountain markets.

What does it cost?

A competitive management rate, which we'll walk through transparently on our call.

How quickly can we launch?

Most furnished, ready homes can be live within a couple of weeks of finalizing the plan.

What if my city restricts short-term rentals?

That's precisely what the assessment is for. We know the regional rules and route you to the play that is both permitted and profitable.
